Formatting tips for a perfect Instant Parse (no AI)
  • One question per numbered line: 1. question text
  • Options on their own lines: (a) … (b) … (same line also works)
  • Marks in brackets at the end: (2) or (2 Marks)
  • Section headings on their own line: SECTION B … — add × 3 = 15 Marks or (3 marks each) for per-question marks, and (Attempt any 5 out of 7 …) for attempt rules
  • True/False: end the statement with [True / False], or put questions under a True or False sub-heading
  • OR choice: keep OR on its own line between the two alternatives
  • Fill-ups: blanks like ______ or …… are detected
  • Answer key at the end is fine — parsing stops there automatically
